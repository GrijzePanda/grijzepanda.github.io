How to Make a Website (aka how this website was made if I didn't know how to make a website)

That's the million-dollar question. Because who wouldn't want a website? Not a stupid one like this, where I yap about how cool I am, but a real one, filled with everything you can imagine. It might seem really hard when you have an empty text editor in front of you, and you might write some tags and end up with something akin to a Word document—boring! Don't give up! There is no need to for it to be hard. Take a deep breath and familiarize yourself with the basic structure of a website: how to make a link, how to place an image, and how to change the size and color of text. Once you have a rough idea, visit your favorite static websites, like the ones on Neocities and STEAL THEIR CODE! Of course, you're not making a copy of their website—that would be lame—but you can use the code as an example. Try ripping out as much of their content as possible until you have nothing but an empty template. Use this to experiment: add, remove, and change divs and tags, move things around to see what the consequences of your actions are. Do the same with the CSS. Once you feel confident enough, you can finally start making your own webite. Take bits and pieces from other websites, from W3C, from Google, or wherever, mash it all together, and customize it until you feel like it's "good enough". It does not have to be perfect; you can always improve your website as your skills improve. Hopefully, you already have an idea of what to put on your website in the first place...
